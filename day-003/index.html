What I tried

The dimensional-drawing skill draws UI components the way Apple draws its devices for accessory makers. I pushed it past its scope: a full drawing set for a modern 12 × 8 m café, using only this skill.

  • Floor plan with 28 seats, kitchen, store, accessible WC, counter with a lowered section, and an accessible route.
  • Four elevations, two building sections, an exploded axonometric in six layers, and counter, booth, entrance and threshold details.
  • Every dimension is a code with its value in a table. The page checks itself: 25 checks that chains add up, seats are counted, aisles are wide enough and nothing overlaps.
  • Open code, accessibility, structure, plumbing and fire items are flagged [VERIFY] for a licensed professional.

Then a harder one: an Indian G+1 house on a 9 × 18 m plot with two 2 BHK flats, a shared stair and a car porch, in 12 sheets.

  • Site plan with area statement, both floor plans, terrace, four elevations, two sections and a stair detail.
  • Door and window schedule counted from the plans, plus a preliminary column grid and plumbing and electrical concepts.
  • 41 checks: stair 2R+T and headroom, car porch clearances, reference room sizes, upper walls standing on lower walls or beams.
  • The checks caught an undersized first-floor lounge, which was then merged into the living room.
